## On the two percentage columns

`rel_error_pct` is `10^MAE - 1`: the typical *multiplicative* error, and the honest headline number. `pct_of_range` divides the GPa error by the full 1-575 GPa span instead - a legitimate normalised-MAE convention, but a much smaller number, because a wide data range shrinks it for free. Quote them together or quote `rel_error_pct`.

**A ~2% relative error is not reachable on this task by anyone.** The labels are DFT-computed elastic moduli, and DFT elastic constants themselves disagree with experiment by roughly 5-15%. A model cannot be more accurate than the data it is fitted to.
